# LIFECRAFT AI — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt
A modern Thai education-technology identity combining calm institutional credibility with energetic classroom growth. Use clean editorial layouts, large Thai typography, rounded evidence cards, subtle depth, and controlled motion. The visual language should feel research-grounded, human-centered, and usable by teachers rather than futuristic or speculative.

## Colors
- `#F2FAF7` — primary light canvas
- `#0E9F8C` — primary teal / actions
- `#0A7A6C` — deep teal / authority
- `#12B48E` — growth mint / positive progress
- `#FFD34D` — learning highlight / attention
- `#6C63C7` — AI coach / reflection
- `#123029` — primary text

## Typography
- Thai: Noto Sans Thai (local rendering substitute for Bai Jamjuree / Sarabun)
- Latin labels: Noto Sans

## Motion
- Confident 0.55–0.70 second scene covers.
- Subtle scale drift during each scene.
- No bouncing, spinning, or decorative motion unrelated to the narrative.
- Transitions must preserve the outgoing frame until the incoming scene covers it.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use generic neon-blue AI visuals.
- Do not use dense dashboard screenshots without a clear focal point.
- Do not imply that AI makes final competency decisions.
- Do not use playful cartoon motion that weakens institutional credibility.
- Do not add claims of proven impact that are absent from the source prototype.
